# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful, assume good faith, criticise the code and not the person, and leave people free to take part
whatever their background or experience.

Unacceptable behaviour can be reported privately through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form or to
the maintainer, [@AF2B](https://github.com/AF2B). Reports are handled with discretion, and the maintainer decides what
follows, up to removing a contribution or a contributor.
